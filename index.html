                I'm a B.Tech CSE student at Uttaranchal University, Dehradun,

                the systems, models, and math that turn raw data into working intelligence.
            


            

                Learning, to me, is closer to engineering than sprinting: break the problem down,
                test the hypothesis, rebuild what fails. I spend most of my time experimenting with

                languages like C++ that push past what's comfortable,
